# Spanish small talk — preparation material

Informal register (**tú**) throughout, as between friends and colleagues of
the same age. Study each section as in any track: read the dialogue aloud,
check only what you did not understand, then rebuild the Spanish from the
English. In the lesson the teacher changes the details on purpose.

---

# Session 1 — Talking about yourself

## Dialogue 1A — Where you are from and what you do

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| — ¿De dónde eres? | — Where are you from? |
| — Soy de Italia, pero vivo aquí desde hace dos años. | — I'm from Italy, but I've lived here for two years. |
| — ¿Y a qué te dedicas? | — And what do you do? |
| — Trabajo en una empresa de software. ¿Y tú? | — I work at a software company. And you? |
| — Soy profesora en un colegio. | — I'm a teacher at a school. |
| — ¿Te gusta tu trabajo? | — Do you like your job? |
| — Sí, bastante. | — Yes, quite a lot. |

## Dialogue 1B — Likes and dislikes

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| — ¿Qué te gusta hacer en tu tiempo libre? | — What do you like doing in your free time? |
| — Me gusta mucho leer y cocinar. ¿Y a ti? | — I really like reading and cooking. And you? |
| — A mí también me gusta cocinar. Y me encanta correr. | — I like cooking too. And I love running. |
| — A mí no me gusta correr. | — I don't like running. |
| — ¿Y madrugar? | — And getting up early? |
| — No me gusta nada madrugar. | — I don't like getting up early at all. |
| — A mí tampoco. | — Me neither. |

## Core patterns

- Soy de … / Vivo en … desde hace … — I'm from … / I've lived in … for …
- Trabajo en / como … — I work at / as …
- (No) me gusta + infinitive / singular noun; (no) me gustan + plural noun.
- Me encanta … — I love …
- ¿Y a ti? — A mí también. / A mí tampoco. / A mí sí. / A mí no.

## You should be able to

- [ ] Say where you are from, where you live and since when.
- [ ] Say what you do and ask the same question back.
- [ ] Say what you like and don't like, and react with también / tampoco.

# Session 2 — The weekend and making plans

## Dialogue 2A — Last weekend

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| — ¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana? | — What did you do at the weekend? |
| — El sábado fui al cine con unos amigos. | — On Saturday I went to the cinema with some friends. |
| — ¿Qué película visteis? | — Which film did you see? |
| — Una comedia. Me gustó mucho. | — A comedy. I liked it a lot. |
| — ¿Y el domingo? | — And on Sunday? |
| — El domingo me quedé en casa. Estaba cansado. | — On Sunday I stayed at home. I was tired. |

## Dialogue 2B — Making plans

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| — ¿Qué vas a hacer esta noche? | — What are you doing tonight? |
| — Voy a cenar con mi familia. | — I'm having dinner with my family. |
| — ¿Quedamos el viernes? | — Shall we meet on Friday? |
| — El viernes no puedo. ¿Qué tal el sábado? | — I can't on Friday. How about Saturday? |
| — Vale, ¿a qué hora? | — OK, what time? |
| — A las ocho en la plaza. | — At eight in the square. |
| — De acuerdo. Hasta el sábado. | — Agreed. See you on Saturday. |

## Core patterns

- Fui / me quedé / vi … — I went / I stayed / I saw … (past, finished)
- Voy a + infinitive — I'm going to …
- ¿Quedamos …? / ¿Qué tal …? — Shall we meet …? / How about …?
- (El viernes) no puedo. — I can't (on Friday).
- A las ocho / a las nueve y media. — At eight / at half past nine.

## You should be able to

- [ ] Say two things you did last weekend.
- [ ] Say what you are going to do tonight or tomorrow.
- [ ] Suggest a day, refuse one and offer another, agree on a time and place.
