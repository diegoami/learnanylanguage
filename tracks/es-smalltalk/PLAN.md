# Plan — Spanish small talk

Two sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons: one repetition
lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B), then one free conversation.
**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode; weak expressions go to the shared Spanish queue
(`queues/es.md` in the learner data), which this track shares with every
other Spanish track.

## Session 1 — Talking about yourself

- **1A Origin and work:** where you are from and live, since when, what you
  do, asking back.
- **1B Likes:** me gusta / me gustan / me encanta, también / tampoco.
- **Covered when:** a self-introduction with work and at least two likes or
  dislikes with a reaction have appeared.

## Session 2 — The weekend and making plans

- **2A Last weekend:** two or three past actions (fui, me quedé, vi).
- **2B Plans:** voy a …, suggesting a day, refusing and offering another,
  agreeing on a time and place.
- **Covered when:** a past-weekend account and an agreed plan with a time
  have appeared.
