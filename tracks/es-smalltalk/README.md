# Spanish small talk (es-smalltalk)

Two short sessions of informal conversation (**tú**): talking about yourself
and your likes, then the weekend and making plans. All dialogues are original.

This track overrides the Spanish profile's classroom phrases in
`track.toml` → `[phrases]`, so the teacher says «Repite, por favor.» and
«Escucha y repite.» instead of the formal «Repita» and «Escuche». It shares
the Spanish review queue with `es-travel`: an expression that stayed weak in
one track may come back in the other.

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md), [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md),
  [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md) — as in every track.
