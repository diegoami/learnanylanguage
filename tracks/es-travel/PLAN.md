# Plan — Spanish for a first trip

Three sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons (about 5–10
minutes): one repetition lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B),
then one free conversation. Every mode counts as practice and as coverage.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included. It is not
mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Spanish review queue
(`queues/es.md` in the learner data) and return later where they fit.

## Session 1 — Greetings and getting by

- **1A Meeting someone:** greet, ask and give a name, origin, residence,
  languages.
- **1B Repair:** not understanding, repetition, slower speech, meaning of a
  word, how to say something.
- **Covered when:** a greeting exchange, a self-introduction with origin and
  languages, and at least three repair expressions have appeared.

## Session 2 — Café, food and paying

- **2A Ordering:** a drink and something to eat, availability, changing the
  order with «mejor …».
- **2B Paying:** the bill, a spoken total, the price of one item, card or
  cash.
- **Covered when:** an order with a change and a payment with a spoken total
  have appeared.

## Session 3 — Directions and transport

- **3A On foot:** asking the way, straight on, left and right, near and far.
- **3B Bus and train:** a bus or train to a place, the stop, a single or
  return ticket, departure time.
- **Covered when:** a walking route with a left/right check and a ticket
  purchase with a time have appeared.
