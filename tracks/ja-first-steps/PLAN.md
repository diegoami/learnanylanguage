# Plan — Japanese first steps

Three sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons (about 5–10
minutes): one repetition lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B),
then one free conversation. The experimental modes (grammar drill,
vocabulary drill, writing) can be added at any point; each session's
**Grammar**, **Vocabulary** and **Writing** sections in `MATERIAL.md` feed
them. Every mode counts as practice and as coverage.

Packages are written in Japanese script only (no romaji): the voice teacher
reads the brief.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included. It is not
mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Japanese review queue
(`queues/ja.md` in the learner data) and return later where they fit.

## Session 1 — Introductions

- **1A Meeting someone:** はじめまして, name, origin (〜から来ました),
  residence (〜に住んでいます), work, よろしくお願いします.
- **1B Repair:** not understanding, once more, slowly, the meaning of a word,
  how to say something in Japanese.
- **Drills:** grammar — です／じゃありません／ですか; vocabulary — jobs,
  languages and repair words; writing — a self-introduction.
- **Covered when:** a self-introduction with origin and work, a question asked
  back, and at least three repair expressions have appeared.

## Session 2 — Shopping and ordering

- **2A Café:** ordering with 〜をください and a number, what something is,
  hot or iced.
- **2B Shop and paying:** the price, sizes (〜はありますか), card, no bag.
- **Drills:** grammar — 〜をください with counters; これ／それ; vocabulary —
  food, drink and shop words; writing — a café order.
- **Covered when:** an order with a counted item and a price question with a
  spoken answer have appeared.

## Session 3 — Time and plans

- **3A Daily life:** the time, getting up, work hours, what you did
  yesterday (ました).
- **3B Plans:** the weekend, inviting with 〜ませんか, accepting with
  〜ましょう, declining with 〜はちょっと….
- **Drills:** grammar — ます／ません／ました／ませんか; vocabulary — times,
  days and activities; writing — your day and an invitation.
- **Covered when:** a time question and answer, one past-tense sentence, and
  an invitation accepted or declined have appeared.
