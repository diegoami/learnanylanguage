# Japanese first steps (ja-first-steps)

Three short sessions of polite everyday Japanese for a first visit to Japan:
introducing yourself and asking for help, shopping and ordering, and talking
about time and plans. The whole track uses the polite **です／ます** forms.
All dialogues are original.

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md): dialogues in Japanese script with romaji and
  translations, patterns, and per session a **Grammar**, a **Vocabulary** and
  a **Writing** section for the drill and writing modes.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): lesson parts, suggested drills, and when a session counts
  as covered.
- [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md): teacher introductions and listen-and-repeat
  lines (copied into packages by `lal`).
- [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md): the empty tracker that `lal start` copies.

In voice mode, start with **«はじめましょう»** (*hajimemashō*); the lesson
ends with «今日のレッスンはこれで終わりです。». A text lesson starts when you
write the same word.

## Track-specific rules

- Packages and openings are written in kanji and kana only: **no romaji and
  no readings in brackets**, because the voice teacher reads the brief aloud.
  Romaji belongs in `MATERIAL.md`, for the learner.
- Keep to です／ます forms; plain forms appear only as dictionary forms in
  the grammar tables.
- Text lessons: the Japanese profile asks the teacher to add the hiragana
  reading after each kanji word the first time it is written. A learner who
  types in romaji should get the answer in Japanese script; that is not an
  error to correct.
- Speech recognition may write the learner's turns in kana or kanji
  differently from the brief; that is not a learner error.
