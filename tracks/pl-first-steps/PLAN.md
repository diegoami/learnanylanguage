# Plan — Polish first steps

Three sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons (about 5–10
minutes): one repetition lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B),
then one free conversation. The experimental modes (grammar drill,
vocabulary drill, writing) can be added at any point; each session's
**Grammar**, **Vocabulary** and **Writing** sections in `MATERIAL.md` feed
them. Every mode counts as practice and as coverage.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included. It is not
mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Polish review queue
(`queues/pl.md` in the learner data) and return later where they fit.

## Session 1 — Greetings and introductions

- **1A Meeting someone:** greeting, name, origin, residence, languages; the
  same questions with pan / pani.
- **1B Repair:** not understanding, repetition, slower speech, the meaning of
  a word, how to say something in Polish.
- **Drills:** grammar — *ja* → *pan / pani* questions; *z* and *w* with
  countries; vocabulary — introductions and repair words; writing — an email
  to a host.
- **Covered when:** a self-introduction with origin and languages, a formal
  question back, and at least three repair expressions have appeared.

## Session 2 — Café, shop and paying

- **2A Ordering:** a drink and something to eat with *poproszę*, size,
  availability (*Czy jest …?*).
- **2B Paying:** the price, card or cash, the bill, together or separately.
- **Drills:** grammar — the accusative after *poproszę*; prices with złoty /
  złote / złotych; vocabulary — café and shop words; writing — an order for
  two.
- **Covered when:** an order with at least one feminine noun in the
  accusative and a payment with a spoken price have appeared.

## Session 3 — Directions and transport

- **3A On foot:** asking the way, straight on, left and right, near and far,
  checking with *Czyli …?*.
- **3B Tram and train:** how to get somewhere, the stop, a ticket,
  departure time.
- **Drills:** grammar — *iść* / *jechać* and the instrumental of vehicles;
  vocabulary — places and transport; writing — directions to your hotel.
- **Covered when:** a walking route with a check, and a transport question
  or ticket purchase with a time have appeared.
