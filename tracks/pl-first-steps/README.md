# Polish first steps (pl-first-steps)

Three short sessions for a beginner's first stay in Poland: greetings and
introductions, the café and paying, directions and transport. With strangers,
staff and officials Polish uses **pan** (to a man) and **pani** (to a woman)
with the third person of the verb; the track does too. All dialogues are
original.

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md): dialogues with translations, patterns, and per
  session a **Grammar**, a **Vocabulary** and a **Writing** section for the
  drill and writing modes.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): lesson parts, suggested drills, and when a session counts
  as covered.
- [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md): teacher introductions and listen-and-repeat
  lines (copied into packages by `lal`).
- [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md): the empty tracker that `lal start` copies.

In voice mode, start with **«Zaczynamy»**; the lesson ends with «Lekcja
skończona.». A text lesson starts when you write the same word.

## Track-specific rules

- The teacher's classroom phrases avoid pan/pani (*Proszę powtórzyć.*), so
  they fit every learner. In guided steps, the teacher addresses the learner
  with **pan** or **pani** as the learner's profile notes say.
- Sentences about the learner in the past tense depend on gender
  (*byłem / byłam*); the openings avoid them, and the material gives both.
- Always write Polish with its diacritics.
- Stress is regular (on the penultimate syllable); add no marks.
