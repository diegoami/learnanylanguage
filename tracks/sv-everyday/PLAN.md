# Plan — Everyday Swedish: first conversations

Three sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons (about 5–10
minutes): one repetition lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B),
then one free conversation. The experimental modes (grammar drill,
vocabulary drill, writing) can be added at any point; each session's
**Grammar**, **Vocabulary** and **Writing** sections in `MATERIAL.md` feed
them. Every mode counts as practice and as coverage.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included. It is not
mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Swedish review queue
(`queues/sv.md` in the learner data) and return later where they fit.

## Session 1 — Introductions and small talk

- **1A Meeting someone:** name, origin, where you live, work, languages; the
  same questions back.
- **1B Repair:** not understanding, repetition, slower speech, the meaning of
  a word, how to say something in Swedish.
- **Drills:** grammar — statements into questions with the verb first;
  vocabulary — jobs, places and repair words; writing — a note for the
  neighbours.
- **Covered when:** a self-introduction with origin and work, questions asked
  back, and at least three repair expressions have appeared.

## Session 2 — Fika, food and likes

- **2A Ordering:** a drink and something to eat, availability, the price,
  paying by card.
- **2B Likes:** likes and dislikes with *tycker om / gillar (inte)*, habits
  with *brukar*, suggesting a fika.
- **Drills:** grammar — en/ett and the definite form, *inte* after the verb;
  vocabulary — café words and prices; writing — the perfect fika.
- **Covered when:** an order with a price and a payment, and a short
  exchange about likes and dislikes have appeared.

## Session 3 — The weekend and plans

- **3A Last weekend:** what you did on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, with the
  past tense and the time expression first.
- **3B Plans:** *ska* + infinitive, inviting with *har du lust att …*,
  accepting and declining.
- **Drills:** grammar — present to past; time expression first (verb second);
  vocabulary — days and free-time activities; writing — last weekend and next
  weekend.
- **Covered when:** a description of a past weekend with at least two past
  verbs, and an invitation accepted or declined have appeared.
