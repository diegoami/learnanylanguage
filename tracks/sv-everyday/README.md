# Everyday Swedish: first conversations (sv-everyday)

Three short sessions for a beginner who lives or works among Swedes:
introducing yourself and asking for help, fika and saying what you like, and
talking about the weekend and making plans. Swedish uses **du** with almost
everyone, so the whole track does. All dialogues are original.

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md): dialogues with translations, patterns, and per
  session a **Grammar**, a **Vocabulary** and a **Writing** section for the
  drill and writing modes.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): lesson parts, suggested drills, and when a session counts
  as covered.
- [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md): teacher introductions and listen-and-repeat
  lines (copied into packages by `lal`).
- [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md): the empty tracker that `lal start` copies.

In voice mode, start with **«Nu börjar vi»**; the lesson ends with
«Lektionen är slut.». A text lesson starts when you write the same words.

## Track-specific rules

- Keep **du** and the informal register in every package.
- Word order is the main grammar thread: the verb comes second (*I lördags
  gick jag …*). Grammar drills should practise it with time expressions first.
- Swedish spelling shows no stress or pitch accent; add no marks.
