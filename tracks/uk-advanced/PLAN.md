# Plan — Advanced Ukrainian: opinions, stories and the news

Three sessions, each worked through as short one-mode lessons (about 5–10
minutes): one repetition lesson, one guided conversation per part (A, B),
then one free conversation. The experimental modes (grammar drill,
vocabulary drill, writing) suit this level especially well; each session's
**Grammar**, **Vocabulary** and **Writing** sections in `MATERIAL.md` feed
them. Every mode counts as practice and as coverage.

Guided conversations at this level are short discussions: the teacher's
lines take a position, and the learner's outcomes are functions (give a
reason, concede, disagree, propose), not fixed sentences.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the accumulated
transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included. It is not
mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Ukrainian review queue
(`queues/uk.md` in the learner data, shared with `uk-reactivation`) and
return later where they fit.

## Session 1 — Opinions and discussion

- **1A Giving an opinion:** state an opinion, give two reasons, weigh two
  sides (*з одного боку … з іншого боку*).
- **1B Disagreeing politely:** partial agreement, disagreement, a
  counter-proposal with *якби*, advice with *я б на вашому місці*, returning
  to the main question.
- **Drills:** grammar — real → unreal conditions; vocabulary — opinion and
  argument words; writing — an opinion piece and a reply.
- **Covered when:** an opinion with a reason, a polite disagreement, and one
  conditional sentence have appeared.

## Session 2 — Telling a story

- **2A Narrating:** a past event in order (*спочатку, потім, зрештою*), with
  background (imperfective) and events (perfective).
- **2B Reacting:** surprise, sympathy, follow-up questions; then the learner
  listens and reacts while the teacher tells a short story.
- **Drills:** grammar — aspect pairs, background + event sentences;
  vocabulary — story connectors and events; writing — an unexpected event.
- **Covered when:** a story of at least four past events with one
  background-and-event sentence, and at least two reactions have appeared.

## Session 3 — News and society

- **3A Reporting:** retelling a news item with *кажуть / пишуть, що …*,
  *як повідомляє …* and -но / -то forms.
- **3B Discussing a problem:** what should be done (*треба / слід / варто*),
  doubt (*я в цьому не впевнений*), a proposal.
- **Drills:** grammar — active → impersonal, direct → reported speech;
  vocabulary — news and society words; writing — a local news item.
- **Covered when:** a retold news item with a reporting phrase and one -но /
  -то form, and a proposal for a problem have appeared.
