# Advanced Ukrainian: opinions, stories and the news (uk-advanced)

Three sessions for a learner who gets by in everyday Ukrainian (for example
after `uk-reactivation`) and wants to argue, narrate and discuss: giving and
defending an opinion, telling a story with the right verb aspect, and
reporting and discussing the news. The register is **ви** throughout. All
texts and dialogues are original.

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md): texts and dialogues with translations, patterns,
  and per session a **Grammar**, a **Vocabulary** and a **Writing** section
  for the drill and writing modes.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): lesson parts, suggested drills, and when a session counts
  as covered.
- [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md): the accented teacher introductions and
  listen-and-repeat lines (copied into packages by `lal`).
- [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md): the empty tracker that `lal start` copies.

The voice-mode start command is **«Почні́мо»**; the lesson ends with
«Заняття закінчено.». This track shares the Ukrainian review queue with
`uk-reactivation`.

## Track-specific rules

- `OPENINGS.md` is fully stress-marked, and the marks were checked with
  `lal audit` (goroh.pp.ua, СУМ-20). `MATERIAL.md` is in ordinary spelling,
  as an advanced learner reads Ukrainian. In packages, copy stress marks from
  the openings and add others only when certain, then run `lal audit`.
- The opening lines use masculine forms (*зго́ден*, *почека́в*,
  *впе́внений*). A learner who uses feminine forms (*зго́дна*, *почека́ла*,
  *впе́внена*) says so in the profile notes; the teacher accepts either.
- Guided conversations here are discussions: the teacher takes the opposite
  view on purpose, so the learner has to defend an opinion.
