# Plan — Ukrainian A1–A2 in 27 topics

The 27 sessions follow the topic map of Є-мова's A1–A2 course «Українська
за 27 уроків», with the same numbering. Sessions 1–3 are phonetics; from
session 4 each session pairs one vocabulary set with one grammar point.
Sessions 1–6 are written; sessions 7–27 are planned here and written in
blocks (the grammar points are this track's own progression).

Each written session is worked through as short one-mode lessons:

- **Phonetics sessions:** repetition lessons (sounds, minimal pairs, stress
  pairs); pronunciation is never judged from transcripts.
- **Theme sessions:** repetition, then a vocabulary drill, one guided
  conversation per part (A, B), a grammar drill, a writing lesson, and a
  free conversation. Skip what the learner does not need.

**Covered** means the session's topic areas have appeared in the
accumulated transcripts of any mode, modelled and cued material included.
It is not mastery: weak expressions go to the shared Ukrainian review queue
(`queues/uk.md`, shared with uk-reactivation and uk-advanced).

## Session 1 — Alphabet and sound distinctions

- **Focus:** phonetics. Letters и, і, ї, є, я, ю, г, ґ, щ, the soft sign and the apostrophe.
- **Lessons:** repetition (minimal pairs кит — кіт, лис — ліс, бик — бік).
- **Covered when:** the three и/і pairs and lines with ї, є, щ, ґ and the apostrophe have been repeated.

## Session 2 — Vowels and stress

- **Focus:** phonetics. Six vowels, unreduced unstressed о, iotated vowels, free and moving stress.
- **Lessons:** repetition (молоко́, за́мок — замо́к, му́ка — мука́).
- **Covered when:** the vowel lines and both stress pairs have been repeated.

## Session 3 — Consonants

- **Focus:** phonetics. г and ґ, в as a short *w*, voiced final consonants, дж and дз, the apostrophe, soft consonants.
- **Lessons:** repetition.
- **Covered when:** lines with г/ґ, final в, final voiced consonants and the apostrophe have been repeated.

## Session 4 — Getting to know someone

- **4A Introducing yourself:** name, origin, residence, languages, with ти.
- **4B Formally:** the same with ви, asking back.
- **Vocabulary:** countries, languages, nationalities, жи́ти, розмовля́ти, вчи́ти.
- **Grammar:** personal pronouns; no *to be* in the present; **з** + genitive and **в / у** + locative of countries.
- **Covered when:** a self-introduction with origin and residence, and questions asked back with ти and with ви, have appeared.

## Session 5 — Greetings, addressing people, polite formulas

- **5A At work:** greetings by time of day, titles, thanks and apologies, with ви.
- **5B With friends:** Приві́т, Як спра́ви, saying goodbye, with ти.
- **Vocabulary:** greetings, thanks, apologies, goodbyes.
- **Grammar:** the vocative case (Андрі́ю, Окса́но, па́не, дру́же); ти and ви.
- **Covered when:** a formal greeting with a vocative, thanks and an answer, and a goodbye have appeared.

## Session 6 — Time, days and months

- **6A The time:** Котра́ годи́на?, half past, о + locative.
- **6B Days and months:** today's day, months with у, seasons, a birthday.
- **Vocabulary:** hours, days, months, seasons.
- **Grammar:** feminine ordinals with годи́на; **о** + locative for times; **у / в** + locative for months.
- **Covered when:** a time question and answer, an *at* time, and a month with у have appeared.

## Session 7 — Weekdays and weekends

- **Vocabulary:** daily routine verbs, the days, free time at the weekend.
- **Grammar:** present tense of the first and second conjugation; **у** + accusative for days (у понеді́лок).

## Session 8 — Weather

- **Vocabulary:** weather, temperature, seasons.
- **Grammar:** impersonal sentences (хо́лодно, йде дощ); the past tense of бу́ти (було́ те́пло).

## Session 9 — My family

- **Vocabulary:** family members, age.
- **Grammar:** possessive pronouns (мій, моя́, моє́, мої́); **у ме́не є** + nominative.

## Session 10 — My profession

- **Vocabulary:** professions, workplaces.
- **Grammar:** the instrumental after працюва́ти and бу́ти (працю́ю лі́карем).

## Session 11 — Parts of the body

- **Vocabulary:** the body.
- **Grammar:** plural of nouns; **у ме́не боли́ть** + nominative.

## Session 12 — Appearance

- **Vocabulary:** height, hair, eyes, describing people.
- **Grammar:** adjective agreement in gender and number; яки́й, яка́, яке́, які́.

## Session 13 — Clothes and sizes

- **Vocabulary:** clothes, colours, sizes, shopping phrases.
- **Grammar:** the accusative of nouns; цей, ця, це, ці.

## Session 14 — My home

- **Vocabulary:** rooms, furniture, renting.
- **Grammar:** **в / на** + locative for places in a home; є and нема́є + genitive.

## Session 15 — Supermarket and food

- **Vocabulary:** groceries, packaging, quantities.
- **Grammar:** the genitive after quantities (пля́шка води́, кілогра́м я́блук); скі́льки.

## Session 16 — Dishes and cooking

- **Vocabulary:** dishes, Ukrainian cuisine, tastes.
- **Grammar:** ї́сти and пи́ти; the accusative of food.

## Session 17 — Restaurant and money

- **Vocabulary:** ordering, the bill, paying.
- **Grammar:** numerals with гри́вня (одна́ гри́вня, дві гри́вні, п'ять гри́вень); я хоті́в би.

## Session 18 — Transport

- **Vocabulary:** means of transport, tickets, stops.
- **Grammar:** the instrumental of transport (ї́хати авто́бусом); іти́ and ї́хати.

## Session 19 — Finding your way in a city

- **Vocabulary:** buildings, directions.
- **Grammar:** prepositions of place with the genitive (бі́ля, навпро́ти, по́руч з); the imperative (іді́ть, поверні́ть).

## Session 20 — Travelling; cities of Ukraine

- **Vocabulary:** travel, cities and regions of Ukraine.
- **Grammar:** the future tense (пої́ду, бу́ду ї́хати, ї́хатиму); **до** + genitive of cities.

## Session 21 — Character and adjectives

- **Vocabulary:** character traits.
- **Grammar:** the comparative of adjectives (ви́щий, кра́щий, розумні́ший).

## Session 22 — Hobbies and free time

- **Vocabulary:** hobbies, sports, culture.
- **Grammar:** verb + infinitive (люблю́ чита́ти); займа́тися + instrumental.

## Session 23 — Services: post office, bank, library

- **Vocabulary:** services and their phrases.
- **Grammar:** the dative (мені́ потрі́бно, надісла́ти ма́мі); тре́ба and мо́жна.

## Session 24 — How you feel

- **Vocabulary:** health, feelings, at the doctor's.
- **Grammar:** dative constructions (мені́ пога́но, йому́ кра́ще); the past tense of feelings.

## Session 25 — State and religious holidays in Ukraine

- **Vocabulary:** holidays, greetings for them.
- **Grammar:** dates in the genitive (пе́ршого сі́чня); віта́ю з + instrumental.

## Session 26 — The nature of Ukraine

- **Vocabulary:** landscape, plants, animals.
- **Grammar:** the locative plural; the superlative (найбі́льший).

## Session 27 — All about studying

- **Vocabulary:** school, university, courses, learning a language.
- **Grammar:** aspect in the past (вивча́в — ви́вчив); plans with збира́тися.
