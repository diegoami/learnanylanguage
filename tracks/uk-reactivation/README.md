# Ukrainian reactivation (uk-reactivation)

A compact review of the functional beginner Ukrainian covered by a typical
Pimsleur Level 1 course, for a learner who finished such a course some time
ago. It is a **review framework**, not a transcript or a lesson-by-lesson
reconstruction of Pimsleur: all dialogues are original.

The aim is to reactivate listening and speaking, find genuine gaps, and avoid
starting another beginner course from the beginning.

## Files

- [MATERIAL.md](MATERIAL.md): what to study before each lesson: complete
  dialogues with translations, patterns, vocabulary, a mastery inventory.
- [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): the seven sessions, their targets and coverage criteria.
- [OPENINGS.md](OPENINGS.md): the accented teacher introduction and
  listen-and-repeat lines of every session (copied into packages by `lal`).
- [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md): the empty tracker that `lal start` copies into
  your learner data.

## Rhythm

| Session | Topic | Suggested timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | diagnostic and repair language | Day 1 |
| 1 | introductions and conversational control | Day 2 |
| 2 | food, shopping, numbers and payment | Day 4 |
| 3 | time, invitations and arrangements | Day 6 |
| 4 | places, movement and directions | Day 9 |
| 5 | people, possessions and daily life | Day 12 |
| 6 | integrated conversation and final check | Day 16 |

Each session is worked through as short one-mode lessons: a repetition
lesson, one guided conversation per lesson part (A, B, C), then a free
conversation. The voice-mode start command is **«Почні́мо»**; the lesson ends
with «Заняття закінчено.».

## Track-specific rules

- Preserve Ukrainian stress marks; add new ones only when certain, and check
  them with `lal audit` (goroh.pp.ua, СУМ-20). Memory proved wrong for
  `хо́чу`, `по́тім` and `ліво́руч`. After a preposition the pronoun keeps the
  forward stress (`у ме́не`, but `мене́`).
- The sample learner is called Діє́го (Diego) in some dialogues and opening
  lines; a learner with another name simply answers with their own.
- Session 0 is a non-gating baseline: after one usable attempt, move on.
