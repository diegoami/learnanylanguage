# Tracker — Ukrainian reactivation (uk-reactivation)

Last updated: —

## What this tracker records

Only vocabulary encountered and Ukrainian sentences or reusable patterns
practised in the copied conversation transcripts. It does not score
comprehension, recall, interaction, grammar, or pronunciation.

Evidence labels describe only what is visible in the transcript:

- **Modelled** — supplied by the teacher.
- **Repeated** — repeated directly after a model.
- **Cued** — produced after a meaningful hint or partial construction.
- **Independent** — produced without the target wording being supplied first.
- **Corrected and repeated** — a preferred form was supplied and then repeated.
- **Needs another turn** — encountered, but the preferred form was not later
  produced in a fresh context.

Pronunciation is not tracked. Transcript spellings are never treated as
pronunciation evidence. Every entry names the mode, the teacher and the prompt
version, and links its `sessions/` folder.
